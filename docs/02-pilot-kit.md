# 02 — Pilot kit (guide task B5)

Ready to print/copy. Prepare these **before** the school visit. Replace the bracketed fields `[…]` each time.

> **Privacy rules for the whole pilot (read once to the class)**
> - We use a **player number only** (shown in the game) — **no names**, no roll numbers, no photos by name.
> - **No accounts, no logins**, no email addresses, no phone numbers.
> - Quiz scores and stars **stay on the device**; the teacher downloads an anonymous CSV.
> - **Photos only with written consent**, and never of children's faces unless the school has consented.
> - One group takes **30–40 minutes**. Keep the groups small so no child waits too long.
> - If a child does not want to play, that is fine — no pressure and no penalty.

---

## (a) Permission letter to the school principal — 1 page (English)

**[Your name(s)]**
[School / college name], [City]
[Phone] · [Email]
20 November 2026

**Subject: Request for a short road-safety game pilot at [School name]**

Dear Principal,

We are two students from [School / college name] building **Road Safety Adventure**, a free browser game that teaches children aged 5–12 how to cross Nepal's roads safely: use the zebra crossing and green man, look right–left–right, stay away from parked vehicles, and never chase a ball onto the road.

We would like to invite **[number] children** from grades **[1–5]** to play the game for **30–40 minutes per group** on **[date]** at **[time]**. A teacher or one of us will be with them the whole time.

**How we protect the children**

- We use a **player number only**. We do not collect names, roll numbers, phone numbers or email addresses.
- There are **no accounts and no logins**. Nothing is sent to the internet.
- Quiz scores and stars are stored **on the device** and exported only as an anonymous CSV.
- We will take **photos only with written consent**, and never of children's faces without school approval.
- A child may stop at any time.

**What we will give the school**

- A short summary of the results (average quiz score before and after, and what children found confusing).
- The teacher feedback form, so you can tell us what to improve.
- A link to the game so the school can keep using it for free.

We will read questions aloud for younger children without hinting, and we will not help unless a child is stuck for more than one minute.

Thank you for considering this request. We are happy to answer any questions before the day.
